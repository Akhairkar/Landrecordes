These tools run in your browser. Your data is not sent anywhere and no login is needed. Each tool states its limits and where to check official records.

{{icon:ruler}}

Land unit converter

Convert between bigha, biswa, katha, acre, hectare, guntha, kanal and square feet. Enter local units yourself.

 {{icon:shape}}

Plot area calculator

Area of a rectangle, triangle, quadrilateral or irregular field from measured sides.

 {{icon:split}}

Partition (batwara) calculator

Split land among co-sharers arithmetically. It does not decide legal entitlement.

 {{icon:search}}

Record finder

From your state, goal and what you have: the right portal and next step, with reasons.

 {{icon:book}}

Land-record glossary

What khatauni, jamabandi, 7/12, intkal are called in 20 states.

 {{icon:check}}

Land purchase checklist

General checks for ownership, mutation, encumbrance, possession; tick and print.

We add only tools whose results can be checked. Where a unit varies by state or district, the tool asks for your local value instead of silently assuming one. Results are always estimates; for registration or legal work the revenue record and the relevant office are what count.

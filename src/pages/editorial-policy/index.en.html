LandRecord pages are prepared and reviewed by the LandRecord editorial team, an independent team not connected to any government department. This page explains how we choose, check and update information.

Sources

  • Every fact (portal address, unit factor, legal section, statistic) is tied to a recorded source. All sources are on the sources page and under "Sources & review" on each article.
  • Priority: government documents (gov.in/nic.in, PIB, court judgments), then official state portals, then supporting sources such as banks or news, which we label "secondary".
  • Where no government source was found we give only general guidance, without section numbers, fees, deadlines or outcomes.

Portal addresses and confidence labels

State pages show a label with each portal address: in a government document, several sources, one source, addresses differ, unconfirmed, or offline only. Until an address is confirmed we don't present it as settled. A failed automated check is not treated as proof that a government site is down; such sites often block outside servers.

What we don't publish

  • Fees, circle rates, stamp-duty rates or local unit sizes such as bigha without a source.
  • Legal outcomes or share decisions for anyone's case.
  • Near-identical district/city pages with only the name changed.
  • Government logos, seals or styling that could make the site look official.

Review and dates

Every article shows a "last reviewed" date. Pages are re-checked when we learn that a rule, portal or process has changed. Automated checks run on every build: sources, titles, duplication, links and accessibility.

AI assistance

Some drafts are written with AI tools. Before publishing, every claim is matched to its source; a claim the source doesn't support is removed or marked as unconfirmed.

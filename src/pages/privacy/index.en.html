This page describes the current situation. If anything changes (for example adding analytics), this page will change first.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for accounts, login or registration.
  • Numbers or names you enter in the tools (calculators, partition, finder, search) stay in your browser and are not sent to our servers.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

What stays in your browser

  • Your theme (light/dark) and language choice.
  • Your ticks in the checklist.

These are stored only in your browser's local storage and can be cleared from browser settings.

Shareable links

Many tools add your entered values to the link (URL) so you can share the result. Before sending a link, check it doesn't contain private information such as a real name.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Ordinary server logs (such as IP address) may be kept under the hosting provider's own policy, which we do not control.

External links

Links to government portals and other sources are subject to their own privacy policies.

Contact

To send suggestions or corrections, open an issue on GitHub.

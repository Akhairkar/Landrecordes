The "Sources & review" section at the bottom of every page says where the information came from and when it was checked.

Types of source

  • Definition: mathematical or standard definitions such as metre and foot.
  • Official: information published by a government portal or department, which gets priority.
  • Secondary: private websites, cited only as supporting references and labelled as such.

When official and other sources give different values, we recommend local confirmation and do not present an estimate as settled.

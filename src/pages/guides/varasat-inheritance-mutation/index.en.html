When a landholder dies, the land does not move to the heirs' names in the revenue record by itself. Entering the heirs' names is called varasat in many states, and elsewhere succession mutation, intkal or ferfar. General steps are below; forms, time limits and officers vary by state.

Why it matters

  • While the record shows the deceased, heirs can't easily sell, mortgage or partition the land.
  • Crop insurance, farmer schemes and the Farmer Registry match names against the record.
  • Delay adds heirs (the next generation) and makes the case harder.

Documents usually needed

  • Death certificate.
  • A current khatauni/jamabandi/7-12 showing the deceased.
  • A list of all heirs with ID and relationship. Many places ask for a family register extract, a panchayat/patwari report or a legal heir certificate.
  • A copy of the will, if any.

General steps

  1. Apply: at the tehsil/revenue office or on the state portal (where online) for inheritance mutation, and keep the acknowledgement. For a structure, use the application drafts tool.
  2. Inquiry: the patwari/lekhpal/talathi verifies the heirs and reports.
  3. Notice and objections: many states publish a notice so objections can be filed.
  4. Order: the competent revenue officer passes an order and the heirs' names are entered.
  5. Check: in the new record, verify each heir's name, father's/husband's name and share.

If it stalls, the steps in mutation pending apply here too.

Common blockers

  • A missing heir: leaving out daughters, children of heirs who died earlier, or children from another marriage is a major cause of disputes. Give a complete list.
  • Name mismatch: different spellings across record, Aadhaar and certificates may need a correction first.
  • An older varasat pending: if land is in the grandfather's name and the father's varasat was never done, that link must be completed first.
  • Objections: family disputes go to a hearing; take a lawyer's advice.

The question of shares

Who inherits and in what share depends on the person's personal succession law, the land category and state law. We don't decide shares here. Once shares are settled, the partition calculator can do the area arithmetic; it does not decide legal entitlement.

Example

Two years after a father's death the khatauni still showed his name. A son applied naming only the two brothers, not their sister; the inquiry found her and the process stopped. The right way was to give the full list of heirs and the family register extract from day one.

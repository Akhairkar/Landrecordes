Most people don't remember the khasra or khata number but do know the owner's name. Many state land-record portals offer search by landholder name. Below: which states have this option in our state-page steps, how to type the name so it is found, and how to pick the right person.

How to type the name

  • Use Hindi where the portal expects it: records are often in Devanagari. Turn on a Hindi keyboard on your phone or use voice typing.
  • Use part of the name: instead of "Ramprasad Yadav", try just "Ramprasad" or "Ram"; many portals list partial matches.
  • Try vowel variants: forms like "Singh/Sinh", "Devi/Dei", "Prasad/Parsad" can differ in the record.
  • Older names: a woman's pre-marriage name or an ancestor's name may be recorded.
  • Pick the right village: the revenue name can differ from the everyday name, and a tehsil may have villages with similar names.

How to identify the right person

  1. Match the father's/husband's name.
  2. Look at the khata number and all khasras in it; is the field you know among them?
  3. Match the area and share.
  4. Once found, note the khata and khasra numbers; every later search is faster with them.

If nothing shows up

  • If inheritance wasn't recorded after the owner's death, the record is in the old name: inheritance guide.
  • If a recent purchase's mutation is pending, the new name won't show yet: mutation stuck.
  • Other ways to find the khasra number: I don't know my khasra number.

Use it responsibly

Name search exists so people can see records of their own land or land they plan to buy. Using someone's land details to threaten, cheat or pressure them is wrong and can bring legal trouble. What the screen shows is for viewing only; for certified copies see online vs certified copy.

Example

Someone searched "Ramprasad Yadav" in English and got nothing. Typing just "रामप्रसाद" with a Hindi keyboard showed three names in the village. Matching the father's name picked the right khata; they spotted their field's khasra in it and noted the khata and khasra numbers.

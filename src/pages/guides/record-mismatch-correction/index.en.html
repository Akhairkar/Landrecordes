Wrongly printed name spellings, father's name, area or khasra number in a khatauni, jamabandi or 7/12 are common. First decide whether the error is clerical or a change that affects someone's rights, because the routes differ.

Compare first

  • Check name, khasra, khata and area in your deed/old records.
  • Take a screenshot/copy of the online record and mark the difference.
  • Compare the name in your ID with the record.

Two broad types of error

TypeExampleUsual route
Clerical/typingSpelling, vowel signs, swapped digitsCorrection application with proof at the revenue office
Real change in rights or areaBig difference in share, holder, areaMutation/revenue court or civil court; consult a lawyer

Which category an error falls in and which officer handles it differ by state, so ask your tehsil office.

What the application usually contains

  • Your name, address, contact; village/tehsil/district.
  • Khasra, khata and a copy of the record showing the error.
  • Proof of the correct details (deed, ID, old records).
  • The correction you want, in one or two clear sentences.

Caution

  • Changes may take time to appear; keep the acknowledgement.
  • Ask about the official process before paying any middleman.
  • If another party's name/share is affected, the matter may become a dispute.

Example

The record shows the father's name as "Ramlal Singh" instead of "Ramlal", or "Ramlaal" instead of "Ramlal". That is usually clerical and corrected by application with proof. Area recorded as 20 bigha instead of 2, or a changed share, is serious and affects other holders, so take advice from a lawyer/revenue court.

Check the unit before comparing

Often the difference is just the unit: the record in hectares and the deed in bigha or square yards. Convert both to one unit with the calculator first.

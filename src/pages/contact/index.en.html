To send a suggestion, correction or site question: open an issue on GitHub. We don't offer email or phone support at present.

What you can write about

  • A wrong or outdated portal address, broken link or incorrect fact (corrections policy).
  • Suggestions for states, tools or guides.
  • Problems using the site.

What we can't help with

  • Fetching anyone's khatauni, jamabandi, 7/12 or map. That happens on the state's official portal or at the tehsil; find your state's route in the record finder.
  • Changing names in records, mutation or settling disputes.
  • Legal advice on anyone's case.

Issues are public. Never post Aadhaar, OTPs, bank details, khasra/khata numbers or private documents. If someone asks for money or an OTP in LandRecord's name, it is not us.

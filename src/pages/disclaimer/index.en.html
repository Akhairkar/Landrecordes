LandRecord is an independent information and tools platform. It is not a government department's website and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government.

What we do

  • Explain land terms, records and processes in simple Hindi and English.
  • Help you reach the right official portal.
  • Provide calculation tools that run in your browser.

What we don't do

  • We do not issue, verify or change land records.
  • We give no opinion on ownership, legal rights or the outcome of any dispute.
  • Tool results are estimates; for registration or legal work the revenue record is what counts.

Fees, rules and processes can change. Confirm with the state's official portal or tehsil office before deciding, and consult a qualified lawyer where needed.

Which record do you need in Nagpur?

  • Rural or agricultural land: Maharashtra uses the 7/12 extract and 8-A as land-record documents. Keep the district, taluka, village and survey/gat number ready.
  • City-surveyed property: A property card (malmatta patrak) and CTS/city-survey number may be relevant. Do not assume every urban address uses the same record; confirm with the local city-survey office.
  • Measurement or boundary-related work: Nagpur district's official land-records service page lists a Measurement Application. Open the current application link from that official page and check the destination carefully.
  • Mutation after inheritance: The district directory links to the state land-records department for legal-heir mutation PDE. Confirm eligibility, documents and status through the official portal or office.

Nagpur district land-records office

The district administration lists the District Superintendent Land Records, Nagpur at Planning and Mining Building, ground floor, beside NIC, Collector Office campus, Civil Lines, Nagpur 440001. The listed phone is 0712-2562948. Before visiting, recheck the official contact directory.

Online view versus a certified copy

Being able to view a record online does not automatically mean that the copy will be accepted by a bank, court or registry office. Maharashtra's portal separates view-only services from digitally signed services. Ask the receiving institution which copy it accepts. Check current fees and availability only on the official portal.

If the record does not appear

  1. Check the revenue name of the district, taluka and village; the portal name can differ from everyday usage.
  2. Where supported, search by survey/gat number or CTS number rather than only by name.
  3. Choose the correct service for rural 7/12 versus an urban property card.
  4. If the record is still missing, contact the Nagpur district land-records office or the relevant taluka office.

In what order

  1. Check the seller's name and share in the land record.
  2. Check the EC, earlier deeds and the mutation chain.
  3. Match possession, boundary and map on the ground; get an official measurement if needed.
  4. Check land use and the master plan.
  5. Estimate the rate and registration cost.
  6. Register, and follow the mutation until it is done.

Online records and this checklist do not guarantee clear title. They don't replace a lawyer's opinion or an official title search; have a qualified lawyer check the papers before a large deal.

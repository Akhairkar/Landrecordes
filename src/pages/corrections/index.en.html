Land portals and rules keep changing, so mistakes can happen. Tell us and we will check and fix them.

How to report

  1. Open an issue on GitHub (a free account is needed).
  2. Give the page link, what is wrong and a source for the correct information (for example a government page).
  3. Don't post your Aadhaar, khasra/khata number, phone or any private document; issues are public.

What we do

  • Match the report against an official source.
  • If it holds, update the page, its source and the "last reviewed" date.
  • Move a portal address's confidence label up or down to match the new evidence.
  • If we can't correct it (for example no source is found), we say why in the issue.

What we cannot do

We cannot see or change anyone's land record. An error in your khatauni/jamabandi is fixed only by the tehsil/revenue office: guide to correcting records.

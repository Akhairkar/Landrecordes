Land records and services are spread across different names and portals in every state. Khasra, khatauni, jamabandi, 7/12, patta chitta: different names, similar purposes. Our job is to connect them in simple language.

Our principles

  • Never present one state's rule as an all-India rule.
  • Show the source and review date with every piece of information.
  • Don't publish what can't be checked: no fees, measurements or legal outcomes we cannot verify.
  • Fewer, useful pages instead of many thin ones.

## Patterns worth copying (functionality, not branding)
1. **1acre.in structure**: per-state *Portal Hub* + *Service Guide* + *Document Guide* + converter + survey-number map layers — a clear hub → guide → document ladder. We mirror the ladder as States → Records → Guides → Tools, with our own sourced content.
2. **Lender blogs** win on authority and breadth but repeat identical steps per state. Our state pages must differ in facts (terms, scope limits, unit habits, cautions).
3. **Proptech converters** disagree on bigha factors; we win on transparency.
